Australian Consumer Law (ACL)

The ACL provides consumer guarantees for goods and services sold in Australia. These guarantees apply automatically and cannot be taken away by a business.

If there is a problem with a product or service, the first step is to contact the business that sold it to discuss a remedy such as repair, replacement, or refund.

In the ACT, Access Canberra is responsible for enforcing the ACL and can assist with consumer rights issues.

Getting Help in the ACT

  • Contact us first so we can try to resolve your concern.
  • Access Canberra can provide consumer information and help with dispute resolution, but it cannot provide legal advice or order compensation.
  • Only a court or tribunal can order compensation or make binding determinations.

Dispute Resolution (ACAT)

The ACT Civil and Administrative Tribunal (ACAT) is an independent tribunal that hears civil disputes, including matters related to contracts, goods, and the ACL.

Privacy

The Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) set standards for how personal information is handled in Australia. Our Privacy Policy explains our practices and how to contact us about privacy issues.

Marketing Communications
